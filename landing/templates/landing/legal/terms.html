The service

Seymy is an app for home-service businesses. It includes:

  • Miss Seymy, an AI assistant that answers your business phone line, takes your customers’ requests and puts them in front of you. She does not book appointments or make commitments on your behalf.
  • Help preparing estimates and invoices, by voice or by typing, for you to review.
  • Optional online payments from your customers through Stripe.

Seymy prepares drafts with AI. Drafts can be wrong. Nothing is sent to your customer until you review and approve it.

Plans and billing

  • Seymy costs $99 per month or $990 per year. There is no free trial.
  • The plan includes one user, the account owner, and 500 Miss Seymy call minutes each month, on monthly and annual plans.
  • Each additional user is a paid seat: $25 per month, or $250 per year on the annual plan. Seymy shows you the price and any prorated amount, and you confirm, before a seat is added.
  • A dedicated Seymy business number and a managed business email are included. There is no setup or activation fee.

Getting paid through Seymy

  • Customer payments through Seymy are optional and processed by Stripe, on your own Stripe connected account.
  • Seymy charges a 1% platform fee on each customer payment collected through Seymy. The fee is not added to what your customer pays.
  • Payments your customers make to you directly, outside Seymy (cash, check, bank transfer), carry no Seymy fee. Creating or sending an estimate is never charged.
