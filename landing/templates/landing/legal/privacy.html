Who we are

Seymy (“Seymy”, “we”, “us”) is the brand of {% include "landing/partials/todo.html" with what="entité légale (raison sociale exacte, forme juridique, État, DBA éventuel)" %}. Seymy provides software for home-service businesses (“Businesses”): an app that helps them prepare estimates and invoices, get paid, and answer their business phone line with an AI assistant, Miss Seymy.

This policy explains what information we process when you visit www.seymy.com, when a Business uses Seymy, when you call a Business that uses Miss Seymy, and in our internal SMS testing program.

Information we collect

  • This website. www.seymy.com has no account, no sign-in and no forms. Like any website, our hosting provider receives the technical data needed to deliver pages, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested.
  • Business account information, provided by a Business when it uses the Seymy app: names, email addresses and phone numbers of the owner and team members, and business details such as the business name and address.
  • Information about a Business’s customers, provided by the Business or by the customer: name, phone number, email address, service address, job details, photos, estimates, invoices and payment status.
  • Calls answered by Miss Seymy: the caller’s phone number, call details (such as time and duration), a written transcript of the conversation and an AI-generated summary. Calls are transcribed; they are not recorded as audio.
  • Payments, processed by Stripe: we receive the payment status and limited details about the payment.
  • App and device information: app version, device type, push notification tokens and technical logs.
  • Internal SMS testing program: the mobile numbers of the Seymy team members who take part, the test messages and their consent.

How we use information

  • To provide Seymy to Businesses: answering their calls, taking their customers’ requests, preparing estimates, invoices and messages for the Business to review, processing payments and sending app notifications.
  • To support Businesses, and to keep Seymy secure and working.
  • To test Seymy’s text messaging with consenting team members (see “Text messages (SMS)”).
  • To meet our legal obligations.

A Business reviews and approves what Seymy prepares before anything is sent to its customers.

AI processing and call confidentiality

Calls answered by Miss Seymy are transcribed through our telephony provider, Twilio, and the speech recognition services it uses. Transcripts, dictation and messages are processed by AI models of our service provider OpenAI to understand requests, summarize and draft content. Miss Seymy keeps a written transcript of each call, not an audio recording. Transcripts and summaries are available to the Business the caller contacted.

  • Resend: email delivery.
