Seymy Internal SMS Testing

Program
Seymy sends non-marketing test text messages only to internal testers who expressly consent in advance. Test messages can simulate customer service conversations, service requests, appointment reminders, quote updates, invoice notifications and payment links. Links in test messages open test pages that use test data only; no real payment is requested or processed. The program does not send promotional messages, messages to the public, or messages on behalf of independent contractor businesses.
How testers opt in
Participation is voluntary. A Seymy representative obtains consent from each tester before their phone number is enrolled. Testers agree to this statement: “I agree to receive test text messages from Seymy at this number. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.” Consent is specific to this testing program and is not a condition of purchasing any service.
Message frequency
Message frequency varies depending on test activity.
Costs
Message and data rates may apply. Seymy does not charge any fee for joining the internal SMS testing program.
Opt out
Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You will receive no further test messages, except a confirmation of your opt-out.
Help
For help with the testing program, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.
Delivery
Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.
