Seymy Internal SMS Testing

Seymy runs one text messaging program today, “Seymy Internal SMS Testing”. It is used only to test how Seymy sends and receives text messages. Messages are sent only to the mobile phones of Seymy team members who have given their explicit consent to receive them. The program does not send text messages to Businesses’ customers or to the public, and it is not a marketing program.
