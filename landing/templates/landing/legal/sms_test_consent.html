About this form

Seymy, Inc. uses this paper form to collect consent from its internal testers before sending them test text messages in the Seymy Internal SMS Testing program. A Seymy representative obtains the tester’s consent on this form before their phone number is enrolled.

Consent for this program is not collected online: this website has no sign-up form and does not collect phone numbers. Seymy retains completed, signed forms privately as evidence of consent; they are never published.

The program, opt-in and disclosure text of the form is the text of the “Text messages (SMS)” section of our Privacy Policy, also published in our Terms & Conditions except for the data-sharing sentence.

Text of the form

SMS Test Recipient Consent

Seymy, Inc. | Seymy Internal SMS Testing | Internal software testing only

Program

Seymy sends non-marketing test text messages only to internal testers who expressly consent in advance. Test messages can simulate customer service conversations, service requests, appointment reminders, quote updates and invoice notifications. The program does not send promotional messages, messages to the public, or messages on behalf of independent contractor businesses.

How testers opt in

Participation is voluntary. A Seymy representative obtains consent from each tester before their phone number is enrolled. Consent is specific to this testing program and is not a condition of purchasing any service.

Important disclosures

  • Message frequency varies depending on test activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Seymy does not charge any fee for joining the internal SMS testing program.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You will receive no further test messages, except a confirmation of your opt-out.
  • Reply HELP for help. For help with the testing program, contact us at rachid@seymy.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Consent - please check the box and sign

☐ I agree to receive test text messages from Seymy at this number. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
