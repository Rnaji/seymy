About this form

Seymy, Inc. uses this paper form to collect consent from its internal testers before sending them SMS test messages in the Seymy Internal SMS Testing program. A Seymy representative obtains the tester’s consent on this form before their phone number is enrolled.

Consent is collected on paper only. This website has no sign-up form and does not collect phone numbers. Seymy retains completed, signed forms privately as evidence of consent; they are never published.

SMS Test Recipient Consent

Seymy, Inc. | Internal software development and QA testing only

Program and messages

Seymy, Inc. sends non-marketing SMS messages to its authorized internal testers to verify SMS delivery and handling in the Seymy software platform. Examples include simulated service updates, quote and invoice notifications, customer-support scenarios, and technical test messages.

Important disclosures

  • Message frequency varies based on testing activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time; reply HELP for assistance.
  • Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase.
  • This program is not used for marketing or for independent contractors’ customers.
