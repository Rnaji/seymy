{# FAQ #}

FAQ

Questions?
Straight answers.

Is Seymy software I have to learn?

No. Just talk or type like you would with a coworker. Seymy handles the software behind the scenes.

Who is Seymy for right now?

Seymy is built for independent contractors and small crews, starting with plumbers.

Will Seymy interrupt me while I’m on a job?

Not unless it really needs you. Seymy handles what it can, saves non-urgent decisions for later, and learns when you prefer not to be interrupted.

Can Seymy really answer my customers?

Miss Seymy answers your business line, takes the customer’s request and puts it in front of you. She doesn’t book appointments or make commitments for you, and nothing goes out to a customer until you approve it.

Does Seymy send estimates without my approval?

No. Seymy prepares the estimate, you review it, and nothing gets sent until you approve it.

What happens when Seymy isn’t sure?

It asks you.

Seymy handles what it knows and brings you in when a decision needs your judgment.

How does pricing work?

Seymy is $99/month or $990/year. You, the owner, are included, with 500 Miss Seymy call minutes each month.

Each additional user is $25/month or $250/year.

Seymy adds a 1% fee only on payments your customers make through Seymy. No feature tiers.

What if my customer pays by cash, check, or bank transfer?

No Seymy fee. Just tell Seymy the payment was received and it keeps the job up to date. The 1% applies only to payments collected through Seymy.
